Paper Piano

Draw instruments on paper. Play them with your fingers.

1

Draw rectangles (piano keys) and circles (drum pads) on white paper with a dark marker.

2

Point your camera at the paper and click Scan Shapes.

3

Press the shapes with your fingertips to play music!

Requires camera & microphone permissions. Works best in Chrome.
